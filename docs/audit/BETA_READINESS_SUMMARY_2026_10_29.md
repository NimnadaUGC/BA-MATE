# BA Mate beta readiness assessment

29 October 2026, Asia/Colombo. This is a current synthesis of the ongoing audit, not a release certification. The detailed chronological evidence, fixes, test qualifications and historical observations are in `END_TO_END_AUDIT_2026_10_29.md` and `FUNCTIONAL_AUDIT_2026_10_29.md`. Historical observations there must be read alongside their later follow-ups; a defect observed early in the audit may already have been repaired.

## Assessment

BA Mate supports a meaningful local journey from reviewed source material through AI proposals, human-edited draft artifacts, evidence links, diagrams and documents. The APNK exercise exposed and helped repair several defects in approvals, revisions, recovery and exports. A simulated project reached later workflow stages, revalidation and baselines, but the simulated answers and verification clicks are not stakeholder validation of APNK. Automated checks and these focused journeys do not establish that every control, provider, operating system or user journey works.

Do not present the real APNK specification as approved, implementation-ready or tested. For beta, resolve the highest-priority reliability and onboarding gaps below and decide explicitly what scope and environments the beta covers. Human review, participant usability and independent security assessment remain necessary.

## Priority work before a broader beta

### 1. Protect user work and make recovery understandable

- **Remaining verification:** Exercise current multi-window conflict handling, source reattachment, partial restore and large or interrupted backups across supported browsers and operating systems. Records-only recovery retains work separately but is not an automatic merge. Existing historical goal lists that were already contaminated are not silently repaired.
- **Recommended:** Offer clear, reviewable recovery and conflict choices, extend the tested APNK v33 backup and same-device restore to supported environments and failure cases, and show which originals or evidence links require review. Measure workspace growth and startup/save time on realistic projects.
- **Evidence already obtained:** A separate synthetic two-window conflict produced a recoverable records-only ZIP; a restore created a distinct project. Matching and mismatched original-file reattachment were exercised separately, including explicit reselection after recovery.

### 2. Make approval and traceability defensible

- **Remaining product risk:** Current checks establish recorded states and paths, not semantic correctness. A simulation reached approval with contradictory requirement/acceptance wording; 100% evidence coverage means a verified path under the implemented metric, not complete or accurate coverage. Baseline approval is self-attested and local; BA Mate does not authenticate the approver or prove stakeholder authority.
- **Recommended:** Require deliberate review of stale document prose and changed decisions; make source-to-requirement coverage and the distinction between verified links, document inclusion and approval understandable. Test multi-goal change revalidation, link editing/removal and historical baseline recovery with realistic records. Decide whether beta needs authenticated identity and authority.
- **Evidence already obtained:** Repairs now require an entered governance rationale, check affected artifacts and dependencies during revalidation, retain immutable baseline content and verify referenced source originals before baseline creation. Positive and negative tests include synthetic baselines; they do not validate the underlying business decisions.

### 3. Improve usable and faithful handover

- **Remaining observed issues:** The current APNK PDF's diagram is small at print size; appendices are dense, and accessible PDF tagging and complete nonvisual diagram descriptions have not been established. The working Word and PDF formats have not been reviewed for every diagram type, long caption, large table, language and page boundary. A document revision does not by itself identify its linked records.
- **Recommended:** Provide readable large or multi-part diagrams, clear document structure and page-level accessibility; validate exports with representative large documents and real viewers. Keep draft, baseline, source and unresolved-decision labels explicit.
- **Evidence already obtained:** Working Markdown, developer handover, Word and PDF download in a live isolated test, with unique per-export IDs matching content and filenames. Baseline export uses the retained snapshot with dependency checks. Source/context records, paragraph breaks, PDF page labels and diagram rendering were repaired and tested in selected cases.

### 4. Make AI review safe and low friction

- **Remaining observed issues:** Live AI proposals included unsupported detail, duplicated questions and an inaccurate process flow; human edits were required. Text-based similarity hints can miss or falsely flag questions. The workbench and chat now recheck source originals before acceptance, but filesystem reads and state changes are not atomic. Not every provider, model, action type, timeout or cancellation case has been exercised live.
- **Recommended:** Keep proposals visibly provisional; show clear evidence, assumptions, gaps and change summaries before adoption. Test long-running, failed, cancelled and stale responses and provider configuration changes with real supported profiles. Simplify model permissions while preserving explicit control of external processing and confidential material. Consider purpose-built evidence selection and source passage traceability rather than asking users to manage long identifier lists.
- **Evidence already obtained:** A live model generated clarification, requirements, diagram, document and quality proposals for APNK; some were corrected or rejected. Source pin enforcement, stale chat proposals, source rechecks and review-time matching have targeted automated or isolated live tests. No new stakeholder answers were invented.

### 5. Improve the analyst journey and accessibility

- **Remaining verification:** The interface has many entry points and dense review screens. A full keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast, mobile/touch and usability study has not been performed. Short guided paths and better empty/error states should be tested with actual analysts rather than assumed effective.
- **Recommended:** Present a clear next step and reason for blockers at each workflow stage; progressively reveal technical controls; use plain labels and consistent terminology for sources, questions, evidence, review and approval. Measure task completion and confusion in moderated beta sessions.
- **Evidence already obtained:** Fixes include compact navigation names and reachable child controls, project/template preview and naming, goal-start notification overlap, project disambiguation, actionable baseline blockers, source search previews, diagram comparisons and clearer recovery messages. These are targeted checks, not an accessibility certification.

### 6. Qualify installation, operations and participant support

- **Remaining verification:** Clean installations and packaged desktop startup across supported systems, upgrades and rollback, staging study-service enrolment/upload, backup limits, security review, representative performance, and provider requalification are incomplete. The study service was observed disconnected; the core local workspace remained available. The production build has large-chunk and third-party directive warnings.
- **Recommended:** Establish a supported OS/browser/Node matrix and reproducible build, install and recovery procedure; verify staging with synthetic participants; run an independent security/privacy review; prepare concise support and data-handling guidance. Reassess dependencies and large bundles before distribution.
