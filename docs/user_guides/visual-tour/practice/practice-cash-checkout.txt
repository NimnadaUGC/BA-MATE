FICTIONAL BETA PRACTICE SOURCE
This example contains no real customer or business data.

Project: Practice Cash Checkout
Purpose: Specify a small cash-only shop checkout.
Stakeholders: Shop owner and cashier.

The cashier enters the order total and cash tendered. The system calculates
the change due. Card payments, refunds and stock management are out of scope.
The shop owner must confirm whether a cash-change limit applies, the limit
amount and the outcome when the limit would be exceeded.

The cash-change limit is intentionally unresolved in this initial brief.
Follow Guide 2 for the separate fictional stakeholder confirmation and
the later controlled-change exercise. Do not treat training instructions
as a real stakeholder's approval.
